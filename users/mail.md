# Mail

Click **Mail** in the navigation bar. The screen has three columns: your
folders, the messages in the chosen folder, and the conversation you opened.

![Mail with the inbox and an open conversation](/img/users/mail-read.png)

The first time you open Mail after your account is created, it may say
**Your mailbox is being prepared**. It appears on its own after a few seconds.

## Folders

The folders are **Inbox**, **Drafts**, **Sent**, **Spam** and **Trash**, plus
**Archive** once you have archived something. A number next to a folder counts
its unread messages (for Drafts, all messages).

New mail arrives without reloading the page.

If you belong to any groups (shared inboxes such as `sales@`), they are
listed under **Groups**, below your own folders. See
[Shared inboxes](./shared-inboxes.md).

## Read a conversation

Click a message in the list. Open Suite shows the whole conversation and
marks it as read. Earlier messages in the conversation are folded; click one
to open it.

The buttons above the conversation are:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Reply | Reply to the sender |
| Reply all | Reply to the sender and everyone else on the message |
| Forward | Forward the last message |
| Archive | Move the conversation to Archive |
| Delete | Move the conversation to Trash. In Trash, it deletes the conversation for ever. |
| Star | Mark the last message with a star |
| Mark unread | Mark it unread and close it |
| Move | Move the conversation to another folder |

**Reply** and **Forward** buttons also sit below the conversation.

Pictures from other websites are hidden to protect your privacy. The message
says **Remote images are hidden to protect your privacy**; click
**Show images** to load them for that message.

Attachments are listed under the message. Click one to download it, or click
the folder icon next to it (**Save to Files**) to keep a copy in Files.

An invitation to an event shows a card with the event's time and place and
**Accept**, **Maybe** and **Decline** buttons. Your answer is sent to the
organiser and the event appears in your calendar. See
[Calendar](./calendar.md#answer-an-invitation).

## Write a message

1. Click **Compose** (or press c). If you can send as a group, choose your
   own address or the group's under **From**.
2. In **To**, type a name or address. Open Suite suggests colleagues, groups
   and your contacts as you type. Separate several addresses with commas. Click
   **Cc Bcc** to add copy recipients.
3. Enter a **Subject** and write the message. The bar below the message has
   **Bold**, **Italic** and **Bulleted list**.
4. To attach files, click the paper clip (**Attach files**), drag files onto
   the message, or click the folder icon (**Attach from Files**) to pick
   something you already keep in Files.
5. Click **Send**, or press Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac).

![Writing a new message](/img/users/mail-compose.png)

The bin icon (**Discard**) throws the message away. The X at the top
(**Save and close**) closes it and keeps it on this device: it opens again the
next time you choose **Write an email** on Home.

:::note Free plan
Organisations on the Free plan can send mail only to addresses hosted on the
same Open Suite installation. Sending to any other address fails with
"Your organisation's plan sends mail only to addresses on Open Suite." Ask
your administrator; see [Plan and limits](/admins/plan-and-limits).
:::

## Search mail

Type in **Search mail** above the message list. Results replace the folder's
messages; clear the box to go back. To search mail together with everything
else, use the main [search](./home-and-search.md#search-everything).

## Keyboard shortcuts

These work when you are not typing in a field:

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| c | Write a new message |
| j / k | Next / previous conversation |
| r | Reply |
| e | Archive |
| # | Delete |
| Ctrl+Enter or Cmd+Enter | Send (while writing) |
