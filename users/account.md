# Your account

Click your initials at the bottom of the navigation bar and choose
**Your account**.

![The account menu](/img/users/account-menu.png)

The page shows your name, address and organisation.

![The Your account page](/img/users/account.png)

## Change your name

Edit **Name** under **Profile** and click **Save**. Colleagues see the new
name in chat, documents and the organisation's contact list.

Your address cannot be changed here. Ask your administrator.

## Change your password

1. Under **Password**, enter your **Current password**.
2. Enter a **New password** of at least 10 characters.
3. Click **Change password**.

Open Suite signs you out on every other device and browser. You stay signed
in on the one you used.

If you have forgotten your password, ask an administrator to reset it. You
receive a temporary password and choose a new one when you sign in.

## Sign out

Click **Sign out** on this page, or in the account menu.
