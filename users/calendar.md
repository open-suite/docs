# Calendar

Click **Calendar** in the navigation bar.

![The calendar in week view](/img/users/calendar-week.png)

The left side has **New event**, a small month for jumping to a date, and
**My calendars**. The top bar has **Today**, the arrows for earlier and later,
and the views **Day**, **Week**, **Month** and **Agenda**. On a narrow screen
**Week** shows three days. Open Suite remembers the view you chose.

## Create an event

The quickest way: click or drag across the time you want in the day or week
view (or click a day in the month view for an all-day event). Type a title in
the small form and click **Save**. Click **More options** for the full form.

For the full form, click **New event** (or press c).

![The New event form with a guest, meeting link, place and reminder](/img/users/calendar-event-editor.png)

1. Enter a title.
2. Set the date and times. Tick **All day** for an event without times. The
   time zone list under the times sets the zone the event is planned in.
3. Choose how it repeats: **Does not repeat**, **Daily**, **Weekly on** a day,
   **Monthly on day** a date, **Monthly on the** first, second, ... or last
   weekday, **Yearly**, **Every weekday (Monday to Friday)**, or **Custom**.
   **Custom** lets you choose every so many days, weeks, months or years and
   which weekdays. Under **Ends**, choose **never**, **on a date** or
   **after** a number of times.
4. Add guests by name or email in **Add guests by name or email**. For
   colleagues, a bar shows when each person is busy that day, from 7:00 to
   21:00, and says whether everyone is free at the time you picked.
5. Click **Add meeting** to attach a video meeting link. See [Meet](./meet.md).
6. Enter a place in **Add a place**.
7. Pick a reminder, from **At the start** to **1 day before**.
8. Add a description, and choose a calendar and a colour if you like.
9. Click **Save**. With guests the button says **Save and invite**, and each
   guest receives an invitation by email.

## View, change or delete an event

Click an event to see its details: time, repeat rule, **Join meeting**,
place, guests with their answers, and description.

![An event's details with the Join meeting button](/img/users/calendar-event-card.png)

- The pencil (**Edit**) opens the full form. When you change an event with
  guests, the button says **Save and notify** and the guests receive the
  update.
- The bin (**Delete**) deletes the event and tells the guests it is cancelled.
  If someone else organised it, the bin is **Remove from my calendar**.
- For a repeating event, Open Suite asks whether to change **This event** or
  **All events**.
- If you organised an event, you can also drag it to a new time, or drag its
  bottom edge to change its length. In the month view, drag it to another day.

The organiser is the only person who can edit or move an event.

## Answer an invitation

Invitations arrive by email. The message shows a card with **Accept**,
**Maybe** and **Decline**. Once the event is in your calendar you can also
answer from its details, where it asks **Going?** with **Yes**, **Maybe** and
**No**. Events you have not answered yet look paler in the calendar.

## Reminders

At the reminder time, a card appears in the corner of whatever screen you
have open, with **Join** for events with a meeting link. If your browser
allows it, click **Show reminders as notifications** under My calendars to
get system notifications too. Reminders only appear while Open Suite is open
in a browser tab.

## Several calendars

Under **My calendars**, click **+** (**New calendar**) to add one, for example
for a project. Tick or untick a calendar to show or hide its events. The
**...** menu next to a calendar has **Name and colour** and
**Delete calendar**, which deletes the calendar and its events. Your first
calendar cannot be deleted.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| c | New event |
| t | Go to today |
| Right arrow, j or n | Later |
| Left arrow, k or p | Earlier |
| d, w, m, a | Day, week, month, agenda view |
