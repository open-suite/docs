# Presentations

Create a presentation with **New presentation** on Home or in Files under
**New**. A new presentation starts with a title slide. Colleagues can edit it
with you at the same time.

![Editing a presentation](/img/users/slides-editor.png)

The slides are listed on the left; click one to edit it, or click **+** under
the list (**Add slide**). Click a placeholder such as **Click to add a title**
and type. Speaker notes go in the box under the slide.

## Add slides and content

The toolbar starts with **Undo**, **Redo** and **Slide**, which adds a new
slide with one of these layouts: **Title**, **Title and body**,
**Two columns**, **Section**, **Picture with caption** and **Blank**.

Then come **Text box**, **Shape** (**Rectangle**, **Rounded rectangle**,
**Ellipse**, **Line**, **Arrow**) and **Picture**.

With nothing selected, the rest of the toolbar sets up the slide:

- **Layout** changes the current slide's layout.
- **Slide background** sets this slide's colour, or **Theme background**.
- **Theme** applies a theme to the whole presentation: Paper, Midnight,
  Ember, Forest, Graphite or Editorial.
- **Transition** between slides: **None**, **Fade** or **Slide**.

With a text box or shape selected, the toolbar shows text tools instead:
**Font**, **Text size**, **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Text colour**,
**Link**, **Align left**, **Centre**, **Align right**, **Vertical position**,
**Bulleted list**, **Numbered list**, **Fill**, **Border**, and **Arrange**
(**Bring to front**, **Bring forward**, **Send backward**, **Send to back**,
the align options, and with three or more items **Distribute across** and
**Distribute down**). Lines and arrows have **Line colour** and **Line ends**.

Right-click an item for **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste**, **Duplicate**,
**Bring to front**, **Send to back** and **Delete**. Right-click a slide in the
list for **New slide**, **Duplicate**, **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste**,
**Present from here** and **Delete slide**.

## Present

![The presenting options](/img/users/slides-present-menu.png)

Click **Present** to start from the first slide (or press F5). The arrow next
to it offers **From the first slide**, **From this slide** and
**With presenter view**.

During the slide show, move forward with the right arrow, Space, Enter,
Page Down or a click, and back with the left arrow, Backspace or Page Up.
Home and End jump to the first and last slide, and Esc ends the show. The
controls at the bottom appear when you move the pointer.

**With presenter view** opens a second window for you with the current and
next slide, your notes (with **Smaller notes** and **Larger notes**), the time
presenting with **Pause timer** and **Reset timer**, and **Previous** and
**Next**. Put the slide show on the screen the audience sees and keep the
presenter window on yours. If the second window does not open, allow pop-ups
for Open Suite in your browser.

## Share, export and import

Click **Share** to share with colleagues; see
[Files and sharing](./files.md#share-with-colleagues).

The **...** menu has:

- **Print or save as PDF**.
- **Download as PowerPoint (.pptx)**.
- **Add slides from PowerPoint…**: adds the slides of a `.pptx` file to this
  presentation.
- **Hide speaker notes** or **Show speaker notes**.
- **Move to trash** (owner only).

To turn a PowerPoint file into a new presentation, use **New** >
**Import Word, Excel or Markdown** in Files, or **Open as presentation** on an
uploaded `.pptx`.

Presentations do not have version history yet, and tables cannot be added to
slides.
