# Find your way around Open Suite

Open Suite uses one sign-in for the portal and the connected apps. The top
navigation is the fastest way to move between tools.

## Use the dashboard

The home page collects your current work in one place:

- **Chat** opens team conversations.
- **Mail** shows unread messages and opens your inbox.
- **Docs** shows recent pages from **More → Wiki**.
- **Calendar** shows upcoming meetings with join links.
- **Meet** shows meeting rooms and lets you start a call.
- **Files** shows recent Office documents and uploaded files.

Use these entries most often:

- **Documents** for text documents.
- **Spreadsheets** for tables and calculations.
- **Presentations** for slide decks.
- **Files** for folders, uploads, sharing, and downloads.

## Pick the right tool

Use this quick map when you know the job but not the app:

| Job | Open this |
| --- | --- |
| Send or read email | **Mail** |
| Write or edit a file | **Office → Documents** or **Office → Files** |
| Upload, share, or download files | **Office → Files** |
| Schedule a meeting | **Calendar** |
| Start or join a video call | **Meet** |
| Find a colleague's details | **More → Contacts** |
| Work with structured records | **More → Tables** |
| Message a team | **Chat** |

## Open the other tools

The remaining top-level links open the other parts of the workspace:

- **Mail** opens your email inbox.
- **Meet** opens video meetings.
- **Chat** opens team chat.
- **Calendar** opens your schedule.
- **More → Tables** opens structured data tables.
- **More → Wiki** opens shared knowledge pages.
- **More → Contacts** opens people and address books.

If a connected app asks you to sign in again, return to the portal and open the
app from the navigation. That refreshes the single sign-on session.
