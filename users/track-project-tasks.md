# Track project tasks

Use Projects for work that needs a shared board: launch plans, team requests,
casework, onboarding, or any workflow that moves through clear stages. Projects
is separate from [Tables](./work-with-tables.md), which is better for registers,
inventories, and structured records.

## Open Projects

Click **Projects** in the Open Suite navigation. The Projects app opens in a new
tab and keeps the same Open Suite header.

The portal's **Projects** widget shows your active boards and card progress. Click
a board there to open it directly.

## Create a project board

1. Click **Add board** in the Projects sidebar.
2. Enter a short project or workflow name.
3. Add lists such as **To do**, **In progress**, and **Done**.
4. Mark the final list as the board's completed list.
5. Add one card for each concrete task.

Keep a card small enough for one person to finish. Split broad outcomes into
several cards so the board still shows what is actually moving.

## Plan and update a task

1. Open a card.
2. Add a description or checklist that makes "done" clear.
3. Assign the person responsible.
4. Add start and due dates when timing matters.
5. Use comments for discussion that should remain attached to the work.
6. Drag the card to the next list when its status changes.

## Keep the board useful

- Keep **In progress** short. Finish, unblock, or reassign work before starting
  more.
- Put decisions and updates on the relevant card instead of scattering them
  across chat and email.
- Review due dates and unassigned cards during team check-ins.
- Archive a board when the project is complete; do not delete it if the history
  is still useful.
