# Join a video call

Use Meet for browser-based video calls. You can join from a calendar event or by
opening Meet from the portal.

## Join from Calendar

1. Open the event in Calendar.
2. Click the Meet URL in the event location.
3. Wait for Meet to open in a new tab.

## Join from Meet

1. Click **Meet** in the top navigation.
2. Click **Join a meeting**.
3. Enter the meeting link or code you received.
4. Confirm to open the room.

## Allow browser permissions

The first time you join, the browser asks for camera and microphone access.
Choose **Allow** so other participants can hear and see you.

If access was blocked, open the browser's site controls beside the address bar,
allow camera and microphone access for Meet, then rejoin the room.

## During the call

Use the toolbar to mute your microphone, turn your camera on or off, share your
screen, open in-call chat, and leave the room. Button placement can change on a
small screen, but each control has an icon and accessible label.

## Before joining

Check these items before a planned meeting:

- Your camera preview looks correct.
- The right microphone is selected.
- The right speaker or headphones are selected.
- Browser notifications or noisy tabs are closed.

## Start an instant meeting

From the portal dashboard, click **Start instant meeting** in the Meet card. Meet
creates a room and opens it immediately. Share the room link with the intended
participants after it opens.

The dashboard also shows **Join** beside upcoming Calendar events that contain a
Meet link.
