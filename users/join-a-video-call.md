# Join a video call

Use Meet for browser-based video calls. You can join from a calendar event or by
opening a Meet link directly.

## Join from Calendar

1. Open the event in Calendar.
2. Click the Meet link in the event location.
3. Wait for Meet to open in a new tab.

## Join from Meet

You can also open **Meet** from the portal and paste a meeting link or room name.

![Meet home page with create and join buttons marked](/img/users/meet-home-annotated.png)

## Allow browser permissions

The first time you join, the browser asks for camera and microphone access.
Choose **Allow** so other participants can hear and see you.

If the browser blocks access, use the camera or lock icon in the address bar to
allow permissions for the Meet site.

## During the call

Use the toolbar to mute your microphone, turn your camera on or off, share your
screen, open chat, and leave the room.

## Before joining

Check these items before a planned meeting:

- Your camera preview looks correct.
- The right microphone is selected.
- The right speaker or headphones are selected.
- Browser notifications or noisy tabs are closed.

## Start an instant meeting

From the portal dashboard, click **Start instant meeting** in the Meet card. Use
this for unscheduled conversations.

![Portal Meet card with start button marked](/img/users/portal-dashboard-annotated.png)

Share the room link with participants after the room opens.

## Join from a calendar event

The portal dashboard shows **Join** next to upcoming events that have a Meet
link. Click **Join** when the meeting is ready.
