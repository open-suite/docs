# Manage your account

Use account settings to update personal details, language, security, and app
preferences.

## Open account settings

1. Open **Office → Files**.
2. Select your avatar in the upper-right.
3. Choose **Settings**. Administrators may see **Personal settings** instead.

## Update personal information

Open **Personal info** and update fields such as your profile picture, full name,
pronouns, email, phone number, or location. Keep these details accurate because
other users can see them when sharing files or searching contacts.

Some fields show a lock or no edit control because they are managed by your
organization. Ask an administrator to correct those values.

## Change language or locale

Under **Personal info**, use **Language**, **Locale**, **First day of week**, and
**Timezone**. Calendar and file timestamps are easier to read when these match
your working location.

## Review security settings

Open **Security** and review **Devices & sessions** when it is available. Revoke
a browser, client, or app-password entry you no longer use or recognize.

Because Open Suite uses single sign-on, password controls can be absent from
this screen. Follow your organization's password-reset process or ask an
administrator instead of creating an app password for browser sign-in.

## Sign out

Return to the portal and click **Log out** in the top navigation. Use it when
working from a shared computer; closing the browser window is not the same as
signing out.
