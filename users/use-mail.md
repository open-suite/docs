# Send and manage email

Use Mail to send and receive email without leaving Open Suite. Your mailbox is
connected automatically when you sign in.

## Open your mailbox

Click **Mail** in the Open Suite navigation. Mail opens your inbox.

## Send an email

1. Click **New message**.
2. Enter an address in **To**, then press **Enter**. Repeat to add another
   recipient. Use **cc** or **bcc** when needed.
3. Enter a short, specific **Subject**.
4. Write your message.
5. Optional: click **Add attachments** or drag files into the attachment area.
6. Check the recipients, subject, message, and attachments, then click **Send**.

Mail saves your work in **Drafts** while you write. You can leave the page and
open the draft later without losing the message.

## Read and reply to email

1. Click a message in the inbox list.
2. Read the conversation in the right-hand panel.
3. Click **Reply** to answer the sender, or **Forward** to send the conversation
   to someone else.
4. Write your message and click **Send**.

Use the actions above a conversation to archive it, mark it as spam, move it to
the trash, mark it unread, or add a label.

## Find and organise messages

- Open **Inbox**, **Drafts**, **Sent**, **Archives**, **Spam**, or **Trash** from
  the left sidebar.
- Use labels for mail that belongs to a team, project, or workflow.
- Use the search field to find a sender, recipient, or words from a message.
- Open **Sent** to confirm that a message was sent.

If expected mail is missing, check **Spam** and **Trash** before asking the
sender to resend it.
