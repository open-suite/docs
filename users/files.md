# Files and sharing

Click **Files** in the navigation bar. Files holds your folders, uploaded
files, and every document, spreadsheet and presentation, side by side.

![Files with the Harbour Coffee folder](/img/users/files-folder.png)

The left side has:

- **My files**: what you own.
- **Shared with me**: what colleagues have shared with you.
- **Recent**: what you used last.
- **Trash**: what you deleted. Items in the trash are deleted for ever after
  30 days.

At the bottom you see how much of your organisation's storage is used.

## Create and upload

Click **New** for:

- **New folder**
- **New document**, **New spreadsheet**, **New presentation**
- **Upload files** and **Upload a folder**
- **Import Word, Excel or Markdown**: turns a `.docx`, `.odt`, `.md`, `.xlsx`,
  `.csv` or `.pptx` file into an Open Suite document, spreadsheet or
  presentation.

![The New menu in Files](/img/users/files-new-menu.png)

You can also click **Upload**, drag files from your computer onto the page,
or paste files. A single file can be up to 2 GB, within your organisation's
storage.

## Work with items

Click a folder to open it, or a document to edit it. Click any other file to
preview it: pictures, video, audio, PDF, text and Markdown show in the
browser; other kinds offer **Download**. In the preview, the arrows move to
the previous and next file and Esc closes it.

![Previewing a picture](/img/users/files-preview.png)

Each item's **...** menu has, depending on the item and your access:
**Download**, **Open as document** (or spreadsheet, or presentation) for Word,
OpenDocument, Markdown, Excel, CSV and PowerPoint files, **Rename** (F2),
**Move to...**, **Share**, **Copy link**, **Select** and **Move to trash**.

To act on several items at once, tick the box at the start of each row (or
Ctrl/Cmd-click, or Shift-click a range). A bar appears with **Download**,
**Move**, **Share** and **Move to trash**. You can also drag items onto a
folder, or onto **My files**, to move them.

Use **Filter by name** to narrow the list, click a column heading to sort, and
switch between **List** and **Grid** with the buttons on the right.

Only the owner can move an item to the trash. In **Trash**, use **Restore** or
**Delete for ever**, or **Empty trash**.

## Share with colleagues

Choose **Share** from an item's menu, from the share icon next to a folder's
name, or with the **Share** button in a document.

![Sharing a folder with everyone in the organisation](/img/users/files-share.png)

- Under **General access**, choose **Only people added** or
  **Everyone at** your organisation.
- To add one person, pick them from **Add a person...** and click **Add**.
  Next to each person choose **Can edit**, **Can comment** (documents only),
  **Can view**, or **Remove**.

Everything inside a shared folder is shared with it. The dialog for an item in
a shared folder says so. Items shared with you appear in **Shared with me**.

![Shared with me, as seen by a colleague](/img/users/files-shared-with-me.png)

## Share a file or folder with anyone

For uploaded files and folders (not documents, spreadsheets or
presentations), the share dialog has **Anyone with the link**. Click
**Create a public link**, then **Copy**. Anyone with the link can view and
download the file, or browse the folder, without an account. Click
**Switch off** to stop the link working.

![A public link for a picture](/img/users/files-public-link.png)

## From Mail and Chat

- In Mail, **Save to Files** next to an attachment keeps a copy in Files, and
  **Attach from Files** adds a file from Files to a message.
- In Chat, **Send from Files** sends a copy of a file into a conversation.
