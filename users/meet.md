# Meet

Meet is for video calls. Every meeting has a permanent link with a code like
`pwr-nkby-rym`. Anyone with the link can join, including people outside your
organisation, who need no account.

Click **Meet** in the navigation bar.

![The Meet screen with your rooms](/img/users/meet-home.png)

## Start or plan a meeting

- **Start a meeting now** creates a room and takes you straight in.
- **Create a room for later** asks for a **Name** (shown to everyone who
  joins), then creates the room and copies its link so you can paste it into
  an email or chat.
- To join someone else's meeting, paste the link or type the code in
  **Enter a code or paste a link** and click **Join**.

You can also start a meeting from **Start a meeting** on Home, from the
**Meet** button in a chat, or add one to a calendar event with **Add meeting**.

**Your rooms** lists the rooms you created. Each has **Copy link**,
**Delete room** and **Join**. A room keeps working until you delete it; after
that its link no longer opens.

## Join

Before you join, you see yourself and can switch the microphone and camera on
or off, and choose a background. Click **Join now**. **Copy link** copies the
meeting link.

![The meeting lobby with camera preview and background choices](/img/users/meet-lobby.png)

If your browser blocks the camera and microphone, the lobby says so. You can
still join and listen. Allow access in your browser's site settings and
reload the page to use them.

## Guests

A guest opens the link in a browser, types **Your name**, and clicks
**Join now**. Guests see the same call screen as everyone else. The browser
remembers the name for next time.

![A guest typing their name before joining](/img/users/meet-guest-lobby.png)

## In the call

![A call with two people](/img/users/meet-call.png)

The top bar shows the meeting name, its code and the number of people. The
buttons at the bottom are:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Microphone | **Mute** or **Unmute** |
| Camera | **Camera off** or **Camera on** |
| Sparkles | **Background**: choose a background effect |
| Screen | **Present your screen**: share a screen, window or tab. Click again to stop. |
| Link | **Copy invite link** |
| Red phone | **Leave** |

When someone presents, their screen fills the stage and everyone's cameras
move to a strip beside it. The person speaking is outlined.

## Backgrounds

Choose a background in the lobby or with the **Background** button during
the call:

- **No effect**
- **Blur slightly** and **Blur**
- six built-in pictures: Studio, Daylight, Dusk, Ocean, Lights and Ember
- **Use your own picture**: a JPEG, PNG or WebP from your device. Remove it
  with the small X on its tile.

The background is worked out on your own device; your camera picture is not
sent anywhere to be processed. Open Suite remembers your choice on that
device for your next call. Your camera must be on to choose one. If your
device's graphics cannot do it, the chooser does not appear or says
**Backgrounds aren't available on this device**.
