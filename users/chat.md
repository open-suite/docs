# Chat

Click **Chat** in the navigation bar. On the left, **Conversations** lists
your chats with the most recent first, and **People** lists colleagues you
have not chatted with yet.

![A conversation in Chat](/img/users/chat.png)

The first time you open Chat it may say **Chat is being prepared**; it
appears on its own after a few seconds. Chat is for people in your
organisation.

## Message a colleague

Click their name under **People** (or under Conversations if you have talked
before). Type in the box at the bottom and press Enter to send. Press
Shift+Enter for a new line.

Links to Open Suite documents and meetings open inside the app. While someone
is typing, the conversation says so, and unread counts show on the
conversation and on **Chat** in the navigation bar.

## Send files and pictures

- Click the paper clip (**Attach a file**), drag files onto the conversation,
  or paste a picture.
- Click the folder icon (**Send from Files**) to send something you keep in
  Files. Chat sends a copy, so everyone in the conversation can open it
  regardless of how the original is shared.

Pictures show in the conversation; click one to open it full size. Other
files show as a download button.

## Group conversations

1. Click **+** (**New group**) next to the Chat heading.
2. Enter a **Name**, tick the **People** to include, and click **Create**.

In any conversation, the **...** menu has **Add people** and
**Leave conversation**.

## Start a meeting from a chat

Click **Meet** at the top of a conversation. Open Suite creates a meeting
room, posts "Join the meeting:" with its link in the conversation, and takes
you into the meeting.
