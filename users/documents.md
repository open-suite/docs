# Documents

Documents are rich-text pages that several people can edit at once. Create
one with **New document** on Home or in Files under **New**. It opens straight
away.

![Editing a document](/img/users/docs-editor.png)

Type the title at the top. Open Suite saves as you type; the label next to
the title says **Saving…**, **Saved**, or **Offline · saved on this device**.
Colleagues editing at the same time appear as avatars at the top right, and
their cursors show in the text with their names.

The arrow at the top left goes back to Files.

## The menus

Under the title are the menus **File**, **Edit**, **View**, **Insert**,
**Format**, **Tools** and **Help**. Everything the editor can do is in them,
with its keyboard shortcut beside it.

![The Insert menu](/img/users/docs-menu.png)

To find something without knowing which menu it is in, press Alt+/ (Option+/
on a Mac) or choose **Help** > **Search the menus**, and type what you want,
such as "footnote" or "landscape".

## Format text

The toolbar has, from left to right: **Undo** and **Redo**; the text style
(**Normal text**, **Heading 1**, **Heading 2**, **Heading 3**); the **Font**
and **Font size**; **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Strikethrough**,
**Text colour** and **Highlight**; **Link**, **Image**, **Table**,
**Footnote** and **Comment**; **Align**, **Line spacing**, **Bulleted list**,
**Numbered list**, **Checklist**, **Decrease indent** and **Increase indent**;
**Clear formatting**; and the mode (see [Suggestions](#suggestions)).

- **Font** offers Arial, Georgia, Times New Roman, Verdana, Trebuchet MS,
  Garamond and Courier New. **Font size** is in points.
- **Align** sets left, centre, right or justified. **Line spacing** is
  single, 1.15 (the default), 1.5 or double.
- **Increase indent** (Ctrl+], Cmd+] on a Mac) moves a paragraph in; in a
  list it nests the item.
- **Format** > **Paragraph style** also has **Quote** and **Code block**;
  **Insert** > **Horizontal line** adds a divider.
- **Clear formatting** (Ctrl+\\) removes fonts, colours, spacing and
  indents from the selection. Comments and suggestions stay.
- Markdown-style typing works too: `# ` starts a heading and `- ` a bulleted
  list.

## Tables and pictures

**Insert** > **Table** (or **Table** in the toolbar) inserts a table of the
size you choose. With the cursor in a table, **Format** > **Table** inserts
and deletes rows and columns, or deletes the table.

To add a picture, click **Image**, or paste or drop a picture into the text.
Click a picture to select it: a bar above it sets its layout and size.

| Layout | The picture |
| --- | --- |
| In line with text | Sits in a line of text, like a large character |
| Break text | Has a line of its own, on the left, centre or right |
| Wrap text, on the left or right | Has text flowing round it |

The sizes are **Small**, **Medium**, **Large**, **Full width** (a quarter,
half, three quarters or all of the text width) and **Original size**. Drag
the picture's corner to size it by hand.

## Page breaks, contents and footnotes

- **Insert** > **Page break** (Ctrl+Enter) starts a new page when the
  document is printed or downloaded. On screen it shows as a dashed line.
- **Insert** > **Table of contents** lists the document's headings, and keeps
  itself up to date as headings are added, changed or removed. Click a
  heading in it to go there.
- **Insert** > **Footnote** (Ctrl+Alt+F) puts a number at the cursor and
  opens a box to write the note in. Notes are listed, numbered, at the end of
  the document. Click a number to change or delete its note.

![Writing a footnote](/img/users/docs-footnote.png)

## Page setup, headers and footers

**File** > **Page setup** sets the paper (A4, Letter, Legal, A5 or A3), the
orientation (portrait or landscape), the margins in millimetres, and a header
and footer. Page setup applies to everyone with the document open.

![Page setup](/img/users/docs-page-setup.png)

The header and footer are printed on every page, on the left, centre or
right. The buttons under each add the **Page number**, the **Page count** and
the **Title**; `Page {page} of {pages}` prints as "Page 3 of 12". On screen
the header and footer show in the margins, with # in place of numbers.

## Find and replace, and word count

**Edit** > **Find and replace** (Ctrl+Shift+H, Cmd+Shift+H on a Mac) opens a
bar over the top of the page; Ctrl+F opens it for finding only. Matches are
highlighted, with the current one in a stronger colour. Press Enter for the
next match and Shift+Enter for the previous. The buttons beside the box
choose **Match case** and **Whole words only**. **Replace** changes the
current match, **Replace all** every one. In **Suggesting** mode the
replacements are suggestions.

![Find and replace](/img/users/docs-find.png)

**Tools** > **Word count** (Ctrl+Shift+C) counts words and characters in the
document and in the selection. The running word count at the bottom right can
be turned off there, or with **View** > **Word count**.

## Comments

1. Select some text.
2. Click **Comment** in the toolbar (or press Ctrl+Alt+M, Cmd+Option+M on a
   Mac).
3. Type your comment and click **Comment**. Type @ and a name to mention a
   colleague.

![Comments and a suggestion beside a document](/img/users/docs-review.png)

Comments appear in the panel on the right, opened with the speech-bubble
button next to **Share** (the number counts open comments and suggestions).
Click a comment to reply with **Reply, or @ a colleague**. The tick
(**Resolve**) closes a thread; **Show resolved** lists closed threads, which
you can **Reopen**. The **...** menu on a comment has **Edit** (your own) and
**Delete**.

## Suggestions

Switch the mode at the right of the toolbar (or in **View**) from **Editing**
to **Suggesting**. What you type and delete is then shown as a proposal: added
text underlined, removed text struck through. Each suggestion appears in the
comments panel, where people who can edit choose **Accept** (tick) or
**Reject** (X). The double tick at the top of the panel offers
**Accept all** *n* **suggestions** and **Reject all suggestions**.

**Viewing** shows the document as it would read with every suggestion
accepted.

People who were given **Can comment** access can comment and suggest, but not
edit directly.

## Version history

Open Suite saves versions as the document changes. If you can edit the
document, choose **File** > **Version history** to see them by day, with who
edited. A version includes its page setup.

![Version history](/img/users/docs-versions.png)

- Click a version to see it. **Back to current** returns to the live
  document.
- **Restore this version** makes it the current version for everyone. What was
  there before is saved as a version first, and Undo reverses the restore.
- **Name this version** gives the current version a name, such as "Sent to
  the board". Named versions are kept for good; unnamed ones are thinned out
  as they age. Rename or name any version from its **...** menu.

## Share

Click **Share**. See [Files and sharing](./files.md#share-with-colleagues) for
the options. Documents can be shared with colleagues; they do not have a
public link.

## Import, export and print

The **File** menu has:

- **Print** (Ctrl+P): the browser's print dialog, where you can also save a
  PDF. Pages print on the paper and with the margins, header and footer from
  page setup; page breaks start new pages. (The header and footer print in
  Chrome, Edge and Safari; Firefox leaves them out.)
- **Download**: **Microsoft Word (.docx)**, **OpenDocument (.odt)**, **PDF**,
  **Web page (.html)** and **Markdown (.md)**. Downloads show the text with
  every suggestion accepted and without comments. Word and OpenDocument files
  keep the fonts, sizes, colours, highlights, line spacing, indents,
  alignment, page breaks, footnotes, contents, paper, margins, header and
  footer.
- **Rename** and **Move to trash** (owner only).

A Word file downloaded from Open Suite comes back with the same formatting
when imported. From other Word and OpenDocument files, Open Suite takes the
text, headings, lists, tables, pictures, links, footnotes, and the page
setup with its header and footer; fonts and colours applied
directly in Word are not taken. A heading's alignment reaches Word but does
not come back from it.

To bring a Word, OpenDocument or Markdown file in, use **New** >
**Import Word, Excel or Markdown** in Files, or **Open as document** on a file
you already uploaded.

## Offline

Documents you have opened on a device are kept on it. If the connection
drops, keep typing: the label says **Offline · saved on this device** and
your changes are sent when you are back online.
