# Documents

Documents are rich-text pages that several people can edit at once. Create
one with **New document** on Home or in Files under **New**. It opens straight
away.

![Editing a document](/img/users/docs-editor.png)

Type the title at the top. Open Suite saves as you type; the label next to
the title says **Saving…**, **Saved**, or **Offline · saved on this device**.
Colleagues editing at the same time appear as avatars at the top right, and
their cursors show in the text with their names.

The arrow at the top left goes back to Files.

## Format text

The toolbar has, from left to right: **Undo** and **Redo**; the text style
(**Normal text**, **Heading 1**, **Heading 2**, **Heading 3**); **Bold**,
**Italic**, **Underline**, **Strikethrough**, **Text colour**, **Highlight**
and **Link**; **Bulleted list**, **Numbered list** and **Checklist**;
**Align left**, **Centre** and **Align right**; **Quote**, **Code block**,
**Divider**, **Image** and **Table**; and **Clear formatting**.

- **Table** inserts a 3 × 3 or 5 × 4 table. With the cursor in a table, the
  same button offers **Add row below**, **Add column right**, **Delete row**,
  **Delete column** and **Delete table**.
- To add a picture, click **Image**, or paste or drop a picture into the text.
- Markdown-style typing works too: `# ` starts a heading and `- ` a bulleted
  list.

The word count is at the bottom right.

## Comments

1. Select some text.
2. Click **Comment** in the toolbar (or press Ctrl+Alt+M, Cmd+Option+M on a
   Mac).
3. Type your comment and click **Comment**. Type @ and a name to mention a
   colleague.

![Comments and a suggestion beside a document](/img/users/docs-review.png)

Comments appear in the panel on the right, opened with the speech-bubble
button next to **Share** (the number counts open comments and suggestions).
Click a comment to reply with **Reply, or @ a colleague**. The tick
(**Resolve**) closes a thread; **Show resolved** lists closed threads, which
you can **Reopen**. The **...** menu on a comment has **Edit** (your own) and
**Delete**.

## Suggestions

Switch the mode at the right of the toolbar from **Editing** to
**Suggesting**. What you type and delete is then shown as a proposal: added
text underlined, removed text struck through. Each suggestion appears in the
comments panel, where people who can edit choose **Accept** (tick) or
**Reject** (X). The double tick at the top of the panel offers
**Accept all** *n* **suggestions** and **Reject all suggestions**.

**Viewing** shows the document as it would read with every suggestion
accepted.

People who were given **Can comment** access can comment and suggest, but not
edit directly.

## Version history

Open Suite saves versions as the document changes. If you can edit the
document, choose **...** > **Version history** to see them by day, with who
edited.

![Version history](/img/users/docs-versions.png)

- Click a version to see it. **Back to current** returns to the live
  document.
- **Restore this version** makes it the current version for everyone. What was
  there before is saved as a version first, and Undo reverses the restore.
- **Name this version** gives the current version a name, such as "Sent to
  the board". Named versions are kept for good; unnamed ones are thinned out
  as they age. Rename or name any version from its **...** menu.

## Share

Click **Share**. See [Files and sharing](./files.md#share-with-colleagues) for
the options. Documents can be shared with colleagues; they do not have a
public link.

## Import, export and print

The **...** menu at the top right has:

![The document's More menu](/img/users/docs-more-menu.png)

- **Print or save as PDF**: opens the browser's print dialog, where you can
  choose to save a PDF.
- **Download as Word (.docx)**, **Download as OpenDocument (.odt)** and
  **Download as Markdown (.md)**. Downloads show the text with every
  suggestion accepted and without comments.
- **Move to trash** (owner only).

To bring a Word, OpenDocument or Markdown file in, use **New** >
**Import Word, Excel or Markdown** in Files, or **Open as document** on a file
you already uploaded.

## Offline

Documents you have opened on a device are kept on it. If the connection
drops, keep typing: the label says **Offline · saved on this device** and
your changes are sent when you are back online.
