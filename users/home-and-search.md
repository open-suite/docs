# Home, navigation and search

## The navigation bar

The dark bar on the left is on every screen. It has **Search**, **Home**,
**Mail**, **Calendar**, **Chat**, **Meet**, **Files** and **Contacts**.
Administrators also see **Admin**. A number on **Mail** or **Chat** counts
unread messages. Your initials at the bottom open a menu with your address,
**Your account**, **Contacts** and **Sign out**.

## Home

Home greets you and shows your organisation's name. Below the greeting are
five buttons: **New document**, **New spreadsheet**, **New presentation**,
**Start a meeting** and **Write an email**. Each opens the new item at once.

The cards below show:

- **Today**: your remaining events for today. **Open calendar** goes to the
  calendar, and **New event** creates one when nothing is planned.
- **Recent documents**: the documents, spreadsheets and presentations you
  used last. **All files** opens Files.
- **Inbox**: your latest mail, with an unread count. **Open mail** opens Mail.
- **Chat**: your latest conversations. **Open chat** opens Chat.
- **Your meeting rooms**: rooms you created. **All meetings** opens Meet.

## Search everything

Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac), press / when you are not typing in a field, or
click **Search** in the navigation bar. Type a few letters. Results appear as
you type, grouped as **People**, **Documents**, **Files**, **Calendar**,
**Contacts**, **Mail**, **Chat** and **Meetings**.

![Search results for "harbour" grouped by kind](/img/users/search.png)

Search looks inside the text of documents, spreadsheets and presentations, not
only at their titles, and it highlights the words it matched.

- Use the arrow keys to move, Enter to open, and Esc to close.
- A person's result offers **Chat with** and **Write to** buttons; a contact
  offers **Write to**.
- Choose **See all results** to open the full search page. There you can
  narrow the results with the tabs (**All**, **People**, **Documents** and so
  on) and choose **Show all** under a group.
- With an empty search box, you see your **Recent searches** and the
  documents you edited recently.
