# Use a shared inbox

A group is a shared inbox with an address of its own, such as `sales@` or an
enquiries address. Anyone can write to it, including people outside your
organisation, unless an administrator has limited who may. Everyone in the
group sees the same mail, so one person can answer and the others see that
it has been dealt with. Administrators set groups up; see
[Groups](/admins/groups).

## Your role in a group

| Role | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Viewer | Read the group's mail. Reading a conversation marks it read for the group. |
| User | Also reply and write as the group, and archive, delete, star and move its mail. |
| Group admin | Also add and remove viewers and users, change their role, and rename the group. |

## Open a group

The groups you belong to are listed in **Mail** under **Groups**, below your
own folders. The number next to a group counts the unread messages in its
inbox. Click the group to open its inbox; its other folders (**Drafts**,
**Sent**, **Archive**, **Spam** and **Trash**) appear below it. New mail
arrives without reloading the page. On a phone, choose the group in the list
above the messages.

## Answer as the group

Open a conversation in the group and click **Reply as** followed by the
group's name (or press r). The message is sent from the group's address, a
copy is kept in the group's **Sent**, and the others in the group see your
name under **Answered by**. Your own address is not shown to the person you
write to.

![Replying to an enquiry from the group's address](/img/users/shared-inbox-reply.png)

To write a new message as the group, open the group and click **Compose**.
When you can send as a group, the message has a **From** line: choose your
own address or a group's. Replies to a group's mail start from the group's
address. Attachments go with the message when you change **From**.

As a viewer you can read the group's mail and **Forward** it from your own
address, but not reply as the group, archive or delete.

## Write to a group

A group's address works like anyone's: type it in **To**. Groups are
suggested as you type and listed in [Contacts](./contacts.md), with what
they are for and who answers them.

If the group takes mail only from its members or from your organisation and
you may not write to it, your message comes back with a note saying who may.
