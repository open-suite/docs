# Running Open Suite

For operators who deploy and maintain the stack: installation, the deploy
pipeline, and backups. Requires root SSH access to the server.

Open Suite is a single-VPS **k3s** distribution that curates the MinBZK
bureaublad portal, La Suite Numérique apps, Nextcloud, and Keycloak behind one
SSO login.
